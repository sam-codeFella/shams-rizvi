I was born in Bhopal. My dad worked for Hindustan Machine Tools, a government job that moved us around, so for a while home was the small hill town of Nainital.

*[Photo: childhood in Nainital]*

I was a curious kid. I wanted to know how the world worked, what other people saw in it, and why they didn't spend every waking hour on the things they loved. I couldn't imagine not doing that.

For me, the first of those things was football.

I played every chance I got. I represented my school in more matches than I can count, and went on to represent Mumbai at the under-14 and under-19 levels, and the Mumbai district side.
